# Analytics Chart Visual System

## Direction

Premium editorial financial dashboard: warm white surfaces, restrained gold accents, clear semantic status colors, low motion, and medium information density.

## Shared Rules

- Chart cards use one radius, border, shadow, title, subtitle, and optional insight treatment.
- Dates use concise Indonesian labels while tooltips preserve context.
- Currency axes use `rb`, `jt`, and `M`; tooltips show full currency values.
- Gold highlights the primary series or top-ranked item only.
- Green communicates positive/healthy data, amber caution/buyback, and red cancellation/risk.
- Line points remain hidden until hover to reduce noise.
- Animation stays between 350–400 ms and respects Chart.js reduced-motion behavior.
- Empty, loading, and error states remain available on every tab.

## Applied Views

- Finance: editorial area-line chart, margin insight, centered transaction total, ranked products and sources.
- Stock: net-movement insight, differentiated in/out series, centered SKU health total, ranked distributions.
- Customer sources: top-channel and repeat-rate insights, ranked channel bars, refined acquisition trend.
- CS team: active-team and top-performer insights, refined stacked trend, progressive ranking colors.
